Privacy

Updated 8 November 2026.

What the service receives

When you ask a study question, the service receives that question, any image you attach and whether you requested web search. When you explicitly enable the MCQ Lens browser extension (Microsoft Edge Add-ons), it captures the visible tab or the question area you select to detect changes. For complete, readable radio-button questions it sends the visible question, code/data and option text for an answer. Otherwise it sends the changed screenshot for reading. Scanning stops when disabled, and pauses while the tab is hidden. The extension does not click or submit answers.

AI providers

Questions may be processed by Z.ai, Groq or Google Gemini. Screenshots are read by Gemini, Groq or Z.ai. Directly extracted page text or the transcribed question and options may be sent to Groq, Z.ai or Gemini for a separate reasoning step that selects the answer. Web-search requests use Google's search grounding and may include search queries derived from your question. Providers apply their own privacy and data-processing terms, including any free-tier data-use terms. Do not send confidential, personal or sensitive information. Selecting a tight question area helps exclude unrelated information.

Storage and retention

Provider API keys stay on the server. A random anonymous ID is stored in your browser and in the extension so the daily allowance works without an account. If you add your own provider keys in the extension, they stay in your browser profile's local extension storage and are sent with your requests only to be used for them; the server does not store them. The server stores per-student and per-provider usage counts and temporary request reservations to enforce allowances. Raw screenshots are processed in memory and are not persisted by this application. Answers, including question text returned by the model, may be cached for up to 24 hours; search-backed answers for up to 5 minutes. Provider-side retention follows provider terms.

Analytics and logs

We count page visits by day and referral tag. Application logs record provider names, question mode and request outcomes, not question bodies, image content, IDs or API keys. Cloudflare infrastructure may process request metadata under its terms. There are no advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data.

Your controls

Turn the extension off, close the study page, delete any keys you saved, or uninstall the extension to stop sending new questions. Avoid including identifying information in questions. AI output can be wrong and should be checked against your course material.

Extension data use

The extension uses page content only to provide its single, explicitly enabled feature: reading the visible multiple-choice question and highlighting a suggested answer. Page content is sent only to the MCQ Lens service and, for that answer, to the AI providers listed above. We do not sell, broker or share it for advertising, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, use the support contact shown on the MCQ Lens listing at the Microsoft Edge Add-ons website.
