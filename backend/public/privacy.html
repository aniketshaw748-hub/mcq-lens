Privacy

Updated 8 November 2026.

What the service receives

When you ask a study question, the service receives that question, any image you attach and whether you requested web search. When you explicitly enable the Chrome extension, it captures the visible tab or the question area you select to detect changes. For complete, readable radio-button questions it sends the visible question, code/data and option text for an answer. Otherwise it sends the changed screenshot for reading. Scanning stops when disabled, and pauses while the tab is hidden. The extension does not click or submit answers.

AI providers

Questions may be processed by Z.ai, Groq or Google Gemini. Screenshots are read by Gemini, Groq or Z.ai. Directly extracted page text or the transcribed question and options may be sent to Groq, Z.ai or Gemini for a separate reasoning step that selects the answer. Web-search requests use Google's search grounding and may include search queries derived from your question. Providers apply their own privacy and data-processing terms, including any free-tier data-use terms. Do not send confidential, personal or sensitive information. Selecting a tight question area helps exclude unrelated information.

Storage and retention

Provider API keys stay on the server. A random anonymous ID is stored in your browser and in the extension so the daily allowance works without an account. If you add your own provider keys in the extension, they stay in your Chrome profile's local extension storage and are sent with your requests only to be used for them; the server does not store them. The server stores per-student and per-provider usage counts and temporary request reservations to enforce allowances. Raw screenshots are processed in memory and are not persisted by this application. Answers, including question text returned by the model, may be cached for up to 24 hours; search-backed answers for up to 5 minutes. Provider-side retention follows provider terms.

Analytics and logs

We count page visits by day and referral tag. Application logs record provider names, question mode and request outcomes, not question bodies, image content, IDs or API keys. Cloudflare infrastructure may process request metadata under its terms. There are no advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data.

Your controls

Turn the extension off, close the study page, delete any keys you saved, or uninstall the extension to stop sending new questions. Avoid including identifying information in questions. AI output can be wrong and should be checked against your course material.

Chrome Web Store use

The extension uses browsing content only to provide the explicitly enabled question-reading and answer-highlighting feature. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Before a public store submission, the publisher must add an owner contact address and review this policy against the deployed service and provider terms.
