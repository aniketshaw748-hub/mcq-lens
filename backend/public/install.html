INSTALL GUIDE

Install MCQ Lens

Pick your browser and follow the steps. It takes about a minute.

Then use it

  1. Open a page with a multiple-choice question. Not sure where? Use the practice test.
  2. Click the MCQ Lens icon, then Enable on this tab.
  3. The suggested option gets a green outline, with the reasoning in a panel at the bottom right. Go to the next question and it answers again.

Each install gets 50 free questions a day. For more, add your own free key in Settings & own keys in the MCQ Lens menu. To update later, download the new zip, replace the old folder’s contents and press the reload button on the extension’s card.

Answers come from AI and can be wrong. Follow your school’s or test platform’s rules.
