MCQ Lens

Settings

Nothing to set up. Turn MCQ Lens on from the toolbar on any question tab. Each browser gets 50 free questions a day.

Your own keys

Optional. With a free key your questions run on your key, with no daily cap. Any one is enough; Groq covers everything.

Ring colour

The ring that marks the suggested option. A dashed ring means the AI is less sure.

Check interval

Bug reports

When something breaks, MCQ Lens sends a short report: what failed, the version, browser, the site's domain and its last steps. Never question text, page content, screenshots or keys.

While on, the question text, or a screenshot of the tab or selected area, goes to the MCQ Lens service and its AI providers (Groq, Google Gemini or Z.ai). Saved keys stay in this browser and are used only for your requests. AI answers can be wrong.
