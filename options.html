◎MCQ LensSETTINGS

Ready to use.

No sign-up, link or key needed. Turn MCQ Lens on from the toolbar on any question tab. The shared service gives each browser 50 questions a day.

Your own keys (optional)

Add a free key if the shared allowance runs out or is busy. Your questions then run on your keys only, with no daily cap from MCQ Lens. Any one key is enough; Groq covers both screenshot reading and answering.

Bug reports

When something breaks, MCQ Lens sends us a short report so we can fix it: what failed, the extension version, browser, the website's domain and the extension's last steps (for example "read page text", "answer in 3 s"). It never includes question text, page content, screenshots or your keys. You can also send a report with a note, and optionally a screenshot, from the "Report a problem" button on the page.

While enabled, the question text, or a screenshot of the visible tab or selected area, goes to the MCQ Lens service and its AI providers (Groq, Google Gemini or Z.ai). Crop out personal information. AI answers can be wrong. Each changed question counts towards the daily allowance.

Saved keys stay in this browser profile's local extension storage. They are sent with your requests to the MCQ Lens service, used only for those requests, and never stored there. Reloading switches scanning off. Each tab must be enabled explicitly.
